# Analytics and Success Metrics

## MVP objective

Validate whether combining guest management and vendor selection creates sustained wedding-planning usage.

## North-star candidates

### Active planned weddings

A wedding is considered active if, within a rolling 14-day period, the couple performs at least two meaningful actions across planning, guest, or vendor workflows.

### Wedding activation rate

Percentage of newly created weddings that complete all three:

1. Add at least 10 guests.
2. Create or confirm at least one event.
3. View or shortlist at least one vendor.

## Funnel metrics

### Couple onboarding

Track:

- `account_created`
- `wedding_created`
- `event_created`
- `guest_added`
- `guest_import_completed`
- `website_published`

### Guest funnel

Track:

- `invite_link_opened`
- `rsvp_started`
- `rsvp_completed`
- `rsvp_updated`

Metrics:

- Invite-to-open rate
- Open-to-RSVP rate
- RSVP completion rate
- Median time to RSVP

### Vendor marketplace funnel

Track:

- `vendor_category_viewed`
- `vendor_profile_viewed`
- `vendor_shortlisted`
- `vendor_enquiry_sent`
- `vendor_quote_sent`
- `vendor_quote_viewed`
- `vendor_selected`

Metrics:

- Profile-to-shortlist rate
- Profile-to-enquiry rate
- Enquiry response rate
- Enquiry-to-quote rate
- Quote-to-selection rate
- Median vendor response time

## Retention signals

Potential weekly active wedding actions:

- Guest list update
- RSVP review
- Checklist completion
- Budget update
- Vendor shortlist
- Enquiry
- Quote review

## Marketplace quality metrics

- Vendors with complete profiles
- Vendors responding within 24 hours
- Enquiries receiving a response
- Vendors receiving repeated enquiries
- Quote expiry without response

## MVP success thresholds

Do not treat these as permanent business targets. They are initial validation thresholds.

Possible early indicators:

- 60%+ of created weddings add 10+ guests
- 40%+ publish a wedding page or send an invitation
- 30%+ browse at least one vendor category
- 15%+ send at least one vendor enquiry
- 60%+ of marketplace enquiries receive a vendor response
- 25%+ of couples who receive a quote mark at least one vendor as shortlisted or selected

## Qualitative research questions

Ask couples:

- What did you use before Ampersand?
- Which part replaced a spreadsheet or chat thread?
- What still requires another tool?
- Did vendor discovery feel useful or distracting?
- What information did you wish vendors already knew?

Ask vendors:

- Was the enquiry sufficiently qualified?
- Was quote creation faster than your current workflow?
- What information was missing?
- Would you maintain availability or packages here?
- Would you recommend Ampersand to couples?
